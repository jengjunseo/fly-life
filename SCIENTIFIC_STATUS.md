## How to read the evidence

The six final conditions use brain seed 20260913 and world seed 20260914, the same
0.5 s baseline and 50 ms control timing. Every post-warmup spike step is computed
on the real full network. A packet's sensory source is the last actual body ACK;
its observed neural output drives the original Godot body code. The next world
snapshot records the resulting position, physiology and new sensory features.

These controlled interventions show **where the implemented chain ends**. They
do not establish all biological synaptic mediation. In particular, a GF response
is not a fly escape, an ORN response is not feeding, and pC1-family activity is not
proof of a female-recognition or exact P1 circuit. Preserved previous sensory-off
and predator-off controls provide separate historical software evidence; they
are not relabeled as new SHOT 4 experiments.

Small target sets are resolved against the actual MaleCNS annotation. LC4 (126),
LPLC2 (185), LC9 (219), food ORN DM1 (74), fly ORN VA1v/VA1d (262), hot TRN VP2
(7), cold VP3a/b (7), Sugar SEL PN (2), Fdg (2), pC1 family (156). GF is DNp01,
not a newly invented type. Exact member bodyIds, aliases and NT values remain in
the mapping artifacts. No P1 selector, NT override or direct central courtship
injection was introduced.
